# Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100)

The document must follow ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Use one word for one meaning, and prefer common, approved words over synonyms.
- Keep procedural sentences to 20 words or fewer and descriptive sentences to 25 words or fewer.
- Give one instruction per sentence.
- Use the active voice, and use the imperative for instructions.
- Use simple tenses; avoid complex verb constructions such as "will have been".
- Do not put more than three nouns together in a row.
- Do not use slang, jargon, idioms, or regional expressions.
- Keep paragraphs short and limited to a single topic.
- Use "must" for requirements and "should" only for recommendations.
